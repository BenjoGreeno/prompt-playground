# 🗣️ What They'll Fire Back — Realistic Replies You'll Hear

You ask a good question, they assume you're fluent, and BANG — a fast reply you didn't
expect. This page gives you **the actual responses locals give** in each scenario, so you
can practise *hearing* them, not just saying your own lines.

Format: **🔊 What they say** — *translation* — **🎯 key word(s) to catch**

> **How to practise:** Have your travel buddy read the Spanish aloud (fast, like a local).
> Your job: catch the **🎯 key word** and figure out the gist. Then swap.

---

## 🚻 Asking where the bathroom is

**You said:** *¿Dónde está el baño?*

**🔊 Replies you'll hear:**

- **Al fondo a la derecha.** — At the back on the right. — 🎯 *fondo* (back), *derecha* (right)
- **Al fondo a la izquierda.** — At the back on the left. — 🎯 *izquierda* (left)
- **Está arriba / abajo.** — It's upstairs / downstairs. — 🎯 *arriba* (up), *abajo* (down)
- **Por ahí, al final del pasillo.** — Over there, at the end of the corridor. — 🎯 *pasillo* (corridor)
- **Tienes que bajar las escaleras.** — You have to go down the stairs. — 🎯 *bajar* (go down), *escaleras* (stairs)
- **Está fuera, en el patio.** — It's outside, in the patio. — 🎯 *fuera* (outside)
- **Sigue todo recto y a la derecha.** — Go straight on and (turn) right. — 🎯 *recto* (straight)
- **Necesitas la llave, pídesela al camarero.** — You need the key, ask the waiter for it. — 🎯 *llave* (key)
- **Está ocupado, hay que esperar.** — It's occupied, you have to wait. — 🎯 *ocupado* (occupied)

**🆘 If you're lost, just say:** *¿Por aquí?* (This way?) + point. They'll nod or redirect.

## ☕ In a café

**You said:** *Un café con leche, por favor.*

**🔊 Replies you'll hear:**

- **¿Grande o pequeño?** — Large or small? — 🎯 *grande* (large), *pequeño* (small)
- **¿Solo o con leche?** — Black or with milk? — 🎯 *solo* (black/alone)
- **¿Normal o descafeinado?** — Regular or decaf? — 🎯 *descafeinado* (decaf)
- **¿Leche fría o caliente?** — Cold or hot milk? — 🎯 *fría* (cold), *caliente* (hot)
- **¿Con azúcar?** — With sugar? — 🎯 *azúcar* (sugar)
- **¿Para tomar aquí o para llevar?** — To have here or take away? — 🎯 *aquí* (here), *llevar* (take)
- **Ahora mismo te lo traigo.** — I'll bring it to you right now. — 🎯 *ahora mismo* (right now)
- **Son tres euros con cincuenta.** — That's €3.50. — 🎯 the number + *euros*
- **¿Algo más? / ¿Algo de comer?** — Anything else? / Anything to eat? — 🎯 *más* (more), *comer* (eat)
- **No tenemos leche de avena, ¿de soja te vale?** — We don't have oat milk, is soy OK? — 🎯 *no tenemos* (we don't have), *soja* (soy)
- **Te lo dejo aquí.** — I'll leave it here for you. — 🎯 *aquí* (here)

**Horchata-specific (Valencia):**
- **¿La quieres natural o granizada?** — Regular or slushie? — 🎯 *granizada* (slushie)
- **¿Quieres fartons para mojar?** — Want fartons for dipping? — 🎯 *fartons*, *mojar* (dip)

## 🍽️ In a restaurant

**You said:** *Una mesa para dos, por favor.*

**🔊 Replies you'll hear:**

- **¿Tienen reserva?** — Do you have a reservation? — 🎯 *reserva*
- **¿Dentro o en la terraza?** — Inside or on the terrace? — 🎯 *dentro* (inside), *terraza*
- **Hay un poco de espera, unos diez minutos.** — There's a bit of a wait, about ten minutes. — 🎯 *espera* (wait), *diez minutos*
- **Síganme, por favor.** — Follow me, please. — 🎯 *síganme* (follow me)
- **¿Esta mesa les va bien?** — Is this table OK for you? — 🎯 *mesa* (table), *bien* (OK)
- **Ahora les traigo la carta.** — I'll bring you the menu now. — 🎯 *carta* (menu)

**Ordering:**
- **¿Qué van a tomar? / ¿Han decidido?** — What will you have? / Have you decided? — 🎯 *tomar* (have), *decidido* (decided)
- **¿De primero, de segundo?** — For starter, for main? — 🎯 *primero* (first), *segundo* (second)
- **¿Y para beber?** — And to drink? — 🎯 *beber* (drink)
- **Eso no queda, lo siento.** — We're out of that, sorry. — 🎯 *no queda* (none left)
- **¿Alguna alergia o intolerancia?** — Any allergy or intolerance? — 🎯 *alergia*
- **Sale enseguida.** — It'll be out right away. — 🎯 *enseguida* (right away)

**Paella-specific (Valencia):**
- **La paella es para mínimo dos personas.** — Paella is for minimum two people. — 🎯 *mínimo dos*
- **Tarda unos veinte minutos, se hace al momento.** — It takes about 20 mins, made fresh. — 🎯 *tarda* (takes time), *veinte minutos*
- **¿La quieren de marisco, mixta o valenciana?** — Seafood, mixed or Valencian? — 🎯 *marisco* (seafood), *valenciana*

**The bill:**
- **¿Junto o separado?** — Together or separate? — 🎯 *junto* (together), *separado*
- **Son cuarenta y dos euros.** — That's €42. — 🎯 the number
- **¿Con tarjeta o en efectivo?** — Card or cash? — 🎯 *tarjeta* (card), *efectivo* (cash)
- **El datáfono no funciona, ¿tiene efectivo?** — Card machine's not working, do you have cash? — 🎯 *no funciona* (not working)

## 🍷 In a bar / tapas place

**You said:** *Dos cañas, por favor.*

**🔊 Replies you'll hear:**

- **Marchando.** — Coming right up. — 🎯 *marchando* (on its way)
- **¿Queréis algo de picar?** — Want something to nibble? — 🎯 *picar* (snack/nibble)
- **¿Os pongo algo de comer?** — Shall I get you something to eat? — 🎯 *comer* (eat)
- **Las cañas vienen con tapa.** — The beers come with a tapa. — 🎯 *vienen con* (come with)
- **¿Qué tapa queréis?** — Which tapa do you want? — 🎯 *qué* (which)
- **Tenemos tortilla, croquetas, jamón…** — We have omelette, croquettes, ham… — 🎯 menu items
- **Hoy tenemos [special] de tapa.** — Today we have [special] as a tapa. — 🎯 *hoy* (today)
- **¿Os cobro ya? / ¿Queréis la cuenta?** — Shall I charge you now? / Want the bill? — 🎯 *cobro* (charge), *cuenta* (bill)
- **Son ocho con cincuenta.** — That's €8.50. — 🎯 the number
- **¿Otra ronda?** — Another round? — 🎯 *otra* (another), *ronda* (round)

**Agua de Valencia-specific:**
- **¿Una jarra o copas individuales?** — A jug or individual glasses? — 🎯 *jarra* (jug)
- **Ojo, que sube bastante.** — Careful, it hits you. — 🎯 *ojo* (watch out), *sube* (goes up/hits)

## 🏨 At the hotel

**You said:** *Tengo una reserva a nombre de Green.*

**🔊 Replies you'll hear:**

- **Muy bien. ¿Me deja su pasaporte?** — Very good. Can I have your passport? — 🎯 *pasaporte*
- **Son dos noches, ¿verdad?** — It's two nights, right? — 🎯 *dos noches*, *verdad* (right?)
- **¿Quieren una habitación con vistas?** — Do you want a room with a view? — 🎯 *vistas* (views)
- **El desayuno es de siete a diez.** — Breakfast is from 7 to 10. — 🎯 *desayuno* (breakfast), the times
- **Está incluido en el precio.** — It's included in the price. — 🎯 *incluido* (included)
- **La habitación estará lista a las tres.** — The room will be ready at 3. — 🎯 *lista* (ready)
- **La contraseña del wifi está en la tarjeta.** — The wifi password is on the card. — 🎯 *contraseña* (password), *tarjeta* (card)
- **El ascensor está al fondo a la izquierda.** — The lift is at the back on the left. — 🎯 *ascensor* (lift)
- **Habitación doscientos cuatro, segunda planta.** — Room 204, second floor. — 🎯 room number, *planta* (floor)
- **Aquí tiene su llave.** — Here's your key. — 🎯 *llave* (key)

**Problems:**
- **Ahora mismo lo miramos.** — We'll look at it right now. — 🎯 *miramos* (we'll look)
- **Le cambiamos de habitación.** — We'll change your room. — 🎯 *cambiamos* (change)
- **Ahora le subimos unas toallas.** — We'll bring you some towels now. — 🎯 *subimos* (bring up), *toallas* (towels)
- **El técnico viene esta tarde.** — The technician is coming this afternoon. — 🎯 *técnico*, *tarde* (afternoon)

**Check-out:**
- **¿La habitación doscientos cuatro?** — Room 204? — 🎯 the number
- **¿Todo bien con la estancia?** — Everything OK with the stay? — 🎯 *estancia* (stay)
- **Sí, pueden dejar las maletas aquí.** — Yes, you can leave your bags here. — 🎯 *maletas* (bags)
- **Se las guardamos hasta las seis.** — We'll keep them for you until 6. — 🎯 *guardamos* (keep), *seis*

## 🏖️ At the beach

**You said:** *¿Se puede alquilar una sombrilla?*

**🔊 Replies you'll hear:**

- **Sí, son diez euros el día.** — Yes, it's €10 for the day. — 🎯 the price, *día* (day)
- **No, esta zona es libre.** — No, this area is free/public. — 🎯 *libre* (free)
- **Tienes que ir al chiringuito de ahí.** — You have to go to the beach bar over there. — 🎯 *chiringuito* (beach bar)
- **¿Sombrilla sola o con hamacas?** — Umbrella only or with sun loungers? — 🎯 *hamacas* (loungers)
- **Lo siento, ya no quedan.** — Sorry, there are none left. — 🎯 *no quedan* (none left)

**Ordering at the chiringuito (beach bar):**
- **¿Qué os pongo?** — What can I get you? — 🎯 *pongo* (get)
- **¿Queréis sentaros o en la barra?** — Want to sit down or at the bar? — 🎯 *sentaros* (sit), *barra* (bar)
- **Os lo llevo a la sombrilla.** — I'll bring it to your umbrella. — 🎯 *llevo* (bring)
- **¿Necesitáis carta?** — Do you need a menu? — 🎯 *carta* (menu)

**Safety / warnings:**
- **Cuidado con la corriente hoy.** — Careful with the current today. — 🎯 *cuidado* (careful), *corriente* (current)
- **No hay socorrista después de las siete.** — There's no lifeguard after 7. — 🎯 *socorrista* (lifeguard)
- **La bandera está roja, no se puede bañar.** — The flag is red, you can't swim. — 🎯 *bandera* (flag), *roja* (red)

- **Hay telonero a las diez.** — There's a support act at 10. — 🎯 *telonero* (support act)
- **Ya ha empezado hace un rato.** — It already started a while ago. — 🎯 *ha empezado* (has started)
- **La puerta abre a las nueve.** — Doors open at 9. — 🎯 *puerta* (doors), *abre* (open)
- **Está lleno, hay que esperar.** — It's full, you have to wait. — 🎯 *lleno* (full), *esperar* (wait)

## 🗺️ Asking for directions (the fast replies!)

**You said:** *¿Dónde está la Plaza del Ayuntamiento?*

**🔊 Replies you'll hear (often rapid-fire!):**

- **Sigue todo recto hasta el semáforo.** — Go straight on to the traffic light. — 🎯 *recto* (straight), *semáforo* (light)
- **Gira a la derecha en la segunda calle.** — Turn right at the second street. — 🎯 *gira* (turn), *segunda* (second)
- **Está al final de esta calle.** — It's at the end of this street. — 🎯 *final* (end)
- **Cruza la plaza y luego a la izquierda.** — Cross the square then left. — 🎯 *cruza* (cross), *luego* (then)
- **Está aquí al lado, a dos minutos.** — It's right here, two minutes away. — 🎯 *al lado* (nearby)
- **Está un poco lejos, mejor coge el metro.** — It's a bit far, better take the metro. — 🎯 *lejos* (far), *metro*
- **No soy de aquí, lo siento.** — I'm not from here, sorry. — 🎯 *no soy de aquí* (not from here)
- **Ni idea, pregunta en ese bar.** — No idea, ask in that bar. — 🎯 *ni idea* (no idea)
- **¿Tienes Google Maps? Es más fácil.** — Do you have Google Maps? It's easier. — 🎯 *más fácil* (easier)

**🆘 Recovery lines when it's too fast:**
- **Perdona, ¿la primera o la segunda?** — Sorry, the first or the second?
- **¿A la derecha?** — To the right? *(repeat what you think you heard as a question)*
- **¿Me lo puedes señalar en el mapa?** — Can you point to it on the map?
- **¿Por aquí?** — This way? *(point + say this — they'll confirm or correct)*

## 🎯 The 10 key words to always listen for

Train your ear to **catch these** — they unlock most replies:

| Word | Means | Sounds like |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **aquí** | here | ah-KEE |
| **ahí / allí** | there / over there | ah-EE / ah-YEE |
| **derecha** | right | deh-REH-chah |
| **izquierda** | left | eeth-kee-ER-dah |
| **arriba / abajo** | up / down | ah-RREE-bah / ah-BAH-hoh |
| **fondo** | back (of room) | FON-doh |
| **no queda / no tenemos** | none left / we don't have | noh KEH-dah / noh teh-NEH-mos |
| **enseguida / ahora mismo** | right away | en-seh-GEE-dah / ah-OH-rah MEES-moh |
| **espera** | wait | es-PEH-rah |
| **incluido** | included | een-kloo-EE-doh |

## 🏋️ Practice drill (do this with your travel buddy!)

1. **Pick a scenario** (café, directions, etc.)
2. **One person reads a Spanish reply** (fast, no warning which one)
3. **Other person catches the key word and guesses the meaning**
4. **Swap roles**

Do this for 10 minutes a day and you'll be catching real replies by the time you land. 🛬🇪🇸
