# 🌍 About Us — "Where are you from?" (personalised)

*"¿De dónde sois?"* is the **#1 question** you'll get from friendly locals. Your situation
is a bit unusual (great conversation fuel!), so here are ready-made answers. Learn the
short version first; pull out the longer bits when you want to keep chatting.

> Grammar note: for **two of you**, locals say *"¿De dónde **sois**?"* (plural).
> To answer about the two of you together, use ***somos*** (we are).
> To talk about just her, use ***ella es*** (she is).

---

## ⚡ The quick answer (learn this cold)

**🔊 ¿De dónde sois?** — Where are you (both) from?

- **Yo soy de Inglaterra, de Nottingham.** — I'm from England, from Nottingham.
  `[yoh soy deh een-glah-TEH-rrah, deh Nottingham]`
- **Mi novia es griega y alemana, pero vive en Inglaterra conmigo.**
  *My girlfriend is Greek and German, but she lives in England with me.*
  `[mee NOH-bee-ah es gree-EH-gah ee ah-leh-MAH-nah, PEH-roh BEE-beh en een-glah-TEH-rrah kon-MEE-goh]`

That's it — that covers 90% of what they want to know. 🎉

---

## 🗣️ If she's answering for herself

- **Soy medio griega, medio alemana.** — I'm half Greek, half German.
  `[soy MEH-dee-oh gree-EH-gah, MEH-dee-oh ah-leh-MAH-nah]`
- **Nací en Alemania pero mi familia es griega.** — I was born in Germany but my family is Greek.
  `[nah-THEE en ah-leh-MAH-nee-ah PEH-roh mee fah-MEE-lee-ah es gree-EH-gah]`
- **Vivo en Inglaterra desde hace años.** — I've lived in England for years.
  `[BEE-boh en een-glah-TEH-rrah DES-deh AH-theh AN-yos]`

---

## 🧩 The building blocks (mix to taste)

**Nationalities (note the -o/-a endings)**
- inglés / inglesa — English (man / woman) `[een-GLES / een-GLEH-sah]`
- griego / griega — Greek `[gree-EH-goh / gree-EH-gah]`
- alemán / alemana — German `[ah-leh-MAN / ah-leh-MAH-nah]`
- británico / británica — British

**Countries & cities**
- Inglaterra — England · Reino Unido — the UK · Nottingham — (say it as-is, they'll get it)
- Grecia — Greece `[GREH-thee-ah]` · Alemania — Germany `[ah-leh-MAH-nee-ah]`

**Handy sentence frames**
- Soy de… — I'm from… · Somos de… — We're from…
- Ella es… — She is… · Vivimos en… — We live in…
- Nací en… — I was born in… · Mi familia es de… — My family is from…
- medio… medio… — half… half…

---

## 💬 Follow-ups they'll ask (and easy replies)

- **🔊 ¿Y vivís juntos?** — And do you live together?
  → *Sí, vivimos juntos en Nottingham.*
- **🔊 ¿Cómo os conocisteis?** — How did you meet? *(you may just catch "conocisteis")*
  → *En Inglaterra.* (keep it simple!) / *Es una larga historia* — "It's a long story" 😄
- **🔊 ¿Habla ella español/griego/alemán?** — Does she speak Spanish/Greek/German?
  → *Habla alemán y griego. Español, un poco, como yo.*
- **🔊 ¿Qué tal se vive en Inglaterra?** — What's it like living in England?
  → *Muy bien, pero hace mucho frío.* — Very good, but it's very cold. 🌧️
- **🔊 ¿Primera vez en Valencia?** — First time in Valencia?
  → *Sí, primera vez, y nos encanta.*

---

## 🎭 Fun bonus — surprise them with Greek/German context

Locals love a bit of colour. Drop one of these and watch them light up:

- **Ella es griega, así que sabe mucho de buena comida.**
  *She's Greek, so she knows a lot about good food.* 🇬🇷
- **En Alemania hace más frío que aquí, ¡esto es el paraíso!**
  *In Germany it's colder than here — this is paradise!* ☀️
- **Valencia y Grecia tienen algo en común: naranjas y sol.**
  *Valencia and Greece have something in common: oranges and sun.* 🍊

> **Why this matters:** answering the "where from?" question confidently is a huge
> confidence boost early in any chat — it's the gateway that gets locals invested in
> talking to you. Nail this and the rest of the conversation flows. 💪
