# 🗣️ Tenses & Talking About Yourself

Single sentences to expand your range — covering the tenses you'll actually use in
conversation, plus personalised sentences about **you** (DevOps engineer, musician, band
member) so you can talk about yourself naturally.

---

## 🎯 The 5 tenses that cover 90% of conversation

You don't need all 14 Spanish tenses. These five get you through almost anything:

| Tense | What it's for | Example |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| **Present** | What you do / like / think | *Trabajo en tecnología* — I work in tech |
| **Near future (ir + a + verb)** | What you're going to do | *Voy a pedir una cerveza* — I'm going to order a beer |
| **Preterite (simple past)** | What you did (completed) | *Ayer fui a la playa* — Yesterday I went to the beach |
| **Imperfect** | What you used to do / was like | *De pequeño tocaba la guitarra* — As a kid I played guitar |
| **Present perfect** | What you've done (recent) | *He comido paella hoy* — I've eaten paella today |

> Focus on **present**, **near future**, and **preterite** first — they're the workhorses.

## 💭 I WANT — Quiero / Quería

**Quiero** = I want (direct) · **Quería** = I'd like (softer, more polite)

| Spanish | English | Say it like |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Quiero una cerveza. | I want a beer. | kee-EH-roh OO-nah ther-BEH-thah |
| Quiero probar la paella. | I want to try the paella. | …proh-BAR lah pah-EH-yah |
| Quiero ver la Ciudad de las Artes. | I want to see the City of Arts. | …ber lah thee-oo-DAD… |
| Quiero aprender más español. | I want to learn more Spanish. | …ah-pren-DER mas es-pan-YOL |
| Quería la cuenta, por favor. | I'd like the bill, please. | keh-REE-ah lah KWEN-tah… |
| Quería reservar una mesa. | I'd like to book a table. | …reh-ser-BAR OO-nah MEH-sah |
| No quiero nada más, gracias. | I don't want anything else, thanks. | noh kee-EH-roh NAH-dah mas |

## 🤔 I THINK — Creo / Pienso

**Creo que…** = I think (that)… · **Pienso que…** = I think (that)… (slightly stronger opinion)

| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| Creo que sí. | I think so. |
| Creo que no. | I don't think so. |
| Creo que está por aquí. | I think it's around here. |
| Creo que he entendido. | I think I understood. |
| Creo que Valencia es preciosa. | I think Valencia is beautiful. |
| Pienso que la paella valenciana es la mejor. | I think Valencian paella is the best. |
| No creo que sea muy lejos. | I don't think it's very far. |

> **Tip:** *Creo que sí* and *Creo que no* are super useful — you'll say them constantly.

## 🚀 I'M GOING TO — Voy a + verb

The easiest way to talk about the future. **Voy a** + infinitive = "I'm going to…"

| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| Voy a pedir una caña. | I'm going to order a beer. |
| Voy a probar el agua de Valencia. | I'm going to try the Agua de Valencia. |
| Voy a visitar el Mercado Central. | I'm going to visit the Central Market. |
| Voy a quedarme una semana. | I'm going to stay for a week. |
| Vamos a cenar fuera esta noche. | We're going to eat out tonight. |
| Vamos a ir a la playa mañana. | We're going to go to the beach tomorrow. |
| No voy a beber más, gracias. | I'm not going to drink any more, thanks. |
| Voy a intentar hablar solo en español. | I'm going to try to speak only in Spanish. |

> **For two of you:** *Vamos a…* = We're going to…

## ⏪ I WENT / I DID — Fui / Hice (Preterite - past)

Completed actions in the past. Key irregular verbs: **ir** (to go) → *fui*, **hacer** (to do) → *hice*

| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| Ayer fui a la playa. | Yesterday I went to the beach. |
| Fui al Mercado Central esta mañana. | I went to the Central Market this morning. |
| Fuimos a un bar muy bueno anoche. | We went to a really good bar last night. |
| Hice una reserva para las nueve. | I made a reservation for 9. |
| ¿Qué hiciste ayer? | What did you do yesterday? |
| Comimos paella en la playa. | We ate paella on the beach. |
| Probé la horchata, ¡me encantó! | I tried the horchata, I loved it! |
| Llegamos ayer por la noche. | We arrived last night. |
| Vi un concierto increíble. | I saw an amazing gig. |

**Time words for past:**
- ayer — yesterday · anoche — last night · esta mañana — this morning
- la semana pasada — last week · hace dos días — two days ago

## 🔄 I USED TO / IT WAS — (Imperfect)

For describing how things were, what you used to do, or background info.

| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| De pequeño tocaba la guitarra. | As a kid I used to play guitar. |
| Antes no me gustaba el vino. | I didn't used to like wine before. |
| Cuando era joven, viajaba mucho. | When I was young, I used to travel a lot. |
| El bar estaba lleno. | The bar was full. |
| Hacía mucho calor ayer. | It was really hot yesterday. |
| No sabía que la paella era de aquí. | I didn't know paella was from here. |

## 💼 I WORK — Trabajo

| Spanish | English | Say it like |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Trabajo en tecnología. | I work in tech. | trah-BAH-hoh en tek-noh-loh-HEE-ah |
| Soy ingeniero de DevOps. | I'm a DevOps engineer. | soy een-heh-nee-EH-roh deh DevOps |
| Trabajo con la nube de Amazon. | I work with Amazon cloud. | …lah NOO-beh deh Amazon |
| Me especializo en AWS. | I specialise in AWS. | meh es-peh-thee-ah-LEE-thoh en AWS |
| Trabajo en infraestructura. | I work in infrastructure. | …een-frah-es-trook-TOO-rah |
| Trabajo desde casa a veces. | I work from home sometimes. | …DES-deh KAH-sah ah BEH-thes |
| Es bastante técnico. | It's quite technical. | es bas-TAN-teh TEK-nee-koh |
| Básicamente, hago que los servidores funcionen. | Basically, I make servers work. | …ser-bee-DOH-res foonk-thee-OH-nen |

**When they ask what DevOps is (they will):**
> *Es una mezcla de desarrollo y operaciones — automatizo cosas para que todo funcione bien.*
> "It's a mix of development and operations — I automate things so everything works well."

## 🎸 I LOVE MUSIC / I PLAY / I'M IN A BAND

| Spanish | English | Say it like |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Me encanta la música. | I love music. | meh en-KAN-tah lah MOO-see-kah |
| Toco varios instrumentos. | I play several instruments. | TOH-koh BAH-ree-ohs eens-troo-MEN-tohs |
| Toco la guitarra. | I play guitar. | …lah gee-TAH-rrah |
| Toco el bajo. | I play bass. | …el BAH-hoh |
| Toco la batería. | I play drums. | …lah bah-teh-REE-ah |
| Toco el piano / el teclado. | I play piano / keyboard. | …el teh-KLAH-doh |
| Estoy en un grupo. | I'm in a band. | es-TOY en oon GROO-poh |
| Tenemos un grupo en Inglaterra. | We have a band in England. | teh-NEH-mos oon GROO-poh… |
| Tocamos [rock / indie / punk / jazz]. | We play [rock / indie / punk / jazz]. | toh-KAH-mos… |
| Hacemos versiones y temas propios. | We do covers and original songs. | ah-THEH-mos ber-see-OH-nes ee TEH-mas PROH-pee-ohs |
| Ensayamos los fines de semana. | We rehearse at weekends. | en-sah-YAH-mos los FEE-nes deh seh-MAH-nah |

**Conversation starters about music:**
| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| ¿Qué música te gusta? | What music do you like? |
| ¿Conoces a [band name]? | Do you know [band name]? |
| ¿Dónde hay buena música en directo? | Where's there good live music? |
| ¿Tocas algún instrumento? | Do you play any instrument? |
| ¿Hay algún garito con bandas locales? | Is there a dive with local bands? |

## 🧩 Putting it together — A mini intro about YOU

Here's a ready-made "about me" you can deliver when chatting:

> **Soy de Nottingham, en Inglaterra. Trabajo en tecnología — soy ingeniero de DevOps,
> me especializo en la nube de Amazon. Pero lo que más me gusta es la música. Toco
> varios instrumentos y estoy en un grupo. Tocamos [rock/indie/etc.]. Mi novia es
> griega y alemana, habla tres idiomas — yo soy un desastre con los idiomas, pero
> lo intento.**

Break it into chunks and practise each bit separately, then string them together.

## 🔁 Quick drills — Mix and match

**Starter** + **middle** + **ending**:

| Starter | Middle | Ending |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Quiero | probar | la paella |
| Voy a | visitar | la playa mañana |
| Ayer fui a | ver | un concierto |
| Creo que | me gusta | este sitio |
| Trabajo en | tecnología | desde hace años |

Pick one from each column → instant sentence. Shuffle and repeat!

## 🎯 The 10 verb forms to know cold

| Infinitive | I (yo) present | I (yo) past | I'm going to |
|------------|---------------|-------------|--------------|
| querer (want) | quiero | quise | voy a querer |
| pensar (think) | pienso | pensé | voy a pensar |
| ir (go) | voy | fui | voy a ir |
| hacer (do/make) | hago | hice | voy a hacer |
| trabajar (work) | trabajo | trabajé | voy a trabajar |
| tocar (play instrument) | toco | toqué | voy a tocar |
| comer (eat) | como | comí | voy a comer |
| beber (drink) | bebo | bebí | voy a beber |
| ver (see) | veo | vi | voy a ver |
| gustar (like) | me gusta | me gustó | me va a gustar |

> **Cheat:** when in doubt, use **voy a + infinitive** for future — it always works and
> sounds natural. Don't worry about the "proper" future tense yet.
