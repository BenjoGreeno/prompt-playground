# 🌙 Arriving Late at the Hotel

You're arriving late-ish, you're tired, you're hungry. Here's everything you need for a
smooth late check-in — especially asking about food options.

---

## 🛎️ Late check-in basics

**🧳 YOU SAY:**

- Buenas noches. Tengo una reserva a nombre de Green.
  *Good evening. I have a reservation under the name Green.*
  `[BWEH-nas NOH-ches. TEN-goh OO-nah reh-SER-bah ah NOM-breh deh Green]`

- Perdone la hora, el vuelo llegó tarde.
  *Sorry about the time, the flight arrived late.*
  `[per-DOH-neh lah OH-rah, el BWEH-loh yeh-GOH TAR-deh]`

- Llegamos tarde porque hubo un retraso.
  *We're arriving late because there was a delay.*
  `[yeh-GAH-mos TAR-deh POR-keh OO-boh oon reh-TRAH-soh]`

**🔊 THEY'LL SAY:**

- **No se preocupe, le estábamos esperando.** — Don't worry, we were expecting you.
- **Tenemos su reserva aquí.** — We have your reservation here.
- **¿Me deja su pasaporte?** — Can I have your passport?
- **Aquí tiene la llave. Habitación [number], [floor] planta.** — Here's the key. Room X, Y floor.

---

## 🍽️ Asking about food (the important bit!)

**🧳 YOU SAY:**

- ¿Se puede comer algo a esta hora?
  *Is it possible to eat something at this time?*
  `[seh PWEH-deh koh-MER AL-goh ah ES-tah OH-rah]`

- ¿El restaurante del hotel sigue abierto?
  *Is the hotel restaurant still open?*
  `[el res-tow-RAN-teh del oh-TEL SEE-geh ah-bee-ER-toh]`

- ¿Tienen servicio de habitaciones?
  *Do you have room service?*
  `[tee-EH-nen ser-BEE-thee-oh deh ah-bee-tah-thee-OH-nes]`

- ¿Hay algún sitio cerca donde comer a estas horas?
  *Is there anywhere nearby to eat at this time?*
  `[eye al-GOON SEE-tee-oh THER-kah DON-deh koh-MER ah ES-tas OH-ras]`

- ¿Conoce algún bar o restaurante que esté abierto todavía?
  *Do you know any bar or restaurant that's still open?*
  `[koh-NOH-theh al-GOON bar oh res-tow-RAN-teh keh es-TEH ah-bee-ER-toh toh-dah-BEE-ah]`

- ¿A qué hora cierra la cocina?
  *What time does the kitchen close?*
  `[ah keh OH-rah thee-EH-rrah lah koh-THEE-nah]`

- Tenemos hambre, ¿hay alguna opción?
  *We're hungry, is there any option?*
  `[teh-NEH-mos AM-breh, eye al-GOO-nah op-thee-ON]`

---

## 🔊 Replies you'll hear (and what they mean)

**Good news:**

- **Sí, el restaurante cierra a las once / doce.** — Yes, the restaurant closes at 11 / 12. 🎯 *cierra* (closes), the time
- **Tenemos servicio de habitaciones hasta medianoche.** — We have room service until midnight. 🎯 *medianoche* (midnight)
- **Le puedo preparar un sándwich / algo ligero.** — I can prepare you a sandwich / something light. 🎯 *sándwich*, *ligero* (light)
- **La cocina está cerrada, pero tenemos snacks en el minibar.** — Kitchen's closed, but there are snacks in the minibar. 🎯 *cerrada* (closed), *minibar*
- **Hay un bar aquí al lado que cierra tarde.** — There's a bar right next door that closes late. 🎯 *al lado* (next door), *tarde* (late)
- **A dos calles hay un kebab / una pizzería abierta.** — Two streets away there's a kebab / pizza place open. 🎯 *dos calles* (two streets)

**Less good news:**

- **Lo siento, la cocina ya está cerrada.** — Sorry, the kitchen is already closed. 🎯 *ya* (already), *cerrada*
- **El restaurante cierra a las diez, lo siento.** — The restaurant closes at 10, sorry. 🎯 the time
- **A esta hora no hay mucho abierto por aquí.** — At this time there's not much open around here. 🎯 *no hay mucho* (not much)
- **Solo tenemos máquinas de snacks.** — We only have snack machines. 🎯 *máquinas* (machines)

## 🎯 Key words to listen for

| Word | Means | Sounds like |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **abierto/a** | open | ah-bee-ER-toh/tah |
| **cerrado/a** | closed | theh-RRAH-doh/dah |
| **la cocina** | the kitchen | lah koh-THEE-nah |
| **hasta las [time]** | until [time] | AS-tah las… |
| **servicio de habitaciones** | room service | ser-BEE-thee-oh deh ah-bee-tah-thee-OH-nes |
| **algo ligero** | something light | AL-goh lee-HEH-roh |
| **cerca / al lado** | nearby / next door | THER-kah / al LAH-doh |
| **todavía** | still | toh-dah-BEE-ah |

## 🍕 Backup plan: asking about late-night food nearby

If the hotel can't help, these are your saviour phrases:

- ¿Hay algún sitio de comida rápida cerca?
  *Is there any fast food place nearby?*

- ¿Dónde puedo comprar algo para comer a esta hora?
  *Where can I buy something to eat at this time?*

- ¿Hay algún supermercado 24 horas por aquí?
  *Is there a 24-hour supermarket around here?*

- ¿Deliveroo / Glovo funciona a esta hora?
  *Does Deliveroo / Glovo work at this time?*
  *(Glovo is big in Spain — worth having on your phone!)*

---

## 🛏️ Other late-arrival questions

- ¿Puedo pedir que me despierten a las [time]?
  *Can I request a wake-up call at [time]?*
  `[PWEH-doh peh-DEER keh meh des-pee-ER-ten ah las…]`

- ¿A qué hora es el desayuno mañana?
  *What time is breakfast tomorrow?*

- ¿Hay agua en la habitación?
  *Is there water in the room?*

- ¿Funciona el aire acondicionado / la calefacción?
  *Does the air con / heating work?*

---

## 🇪🇸 Valencia late-night food context

- **Dinner is late in Spain** — locals often eat at 9–10pm, so restaurants stay open later than you'd expect (often until 11pm–midnight, especially on weekends).
- **Tapas bars** are often your best bet for late food — many stay open until 1am+.
- **Kebab / pizza / fast food** places near the centre are usually open very late.
- **Glovo** (like Deliveroo) delivers late and is widely used in Valencia.
- Worst case: look for a **gasolinera** (petrol station) with a shop — they're often 24h.

> **Pro tip:** If the receptionist says *"la cocina está cerrada"* (kitchen's closed),
> immediately follow up with: *"¿Conoce algún sitio cerca que esté abierto?"* — they'll
> almost always point you somewhere. Hotel staff know the area! 😊
