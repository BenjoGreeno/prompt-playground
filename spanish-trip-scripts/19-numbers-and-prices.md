## 🔢 31–99 (the "y" pattern)

From 31 onwards, it's [tens] **y** [units] — "thirty and one", etc.

| # | Spanish | Pattern |
|---|---------|---------|

## 💶 How prices are said

**"Son [number] euros"** — That's [X] euros.
**"Son [number] con [cents]"** — That's [X] euros and [Y] cents.

| They say | They mean | Sounds like |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Son tres euros | €3.00 | son tres EH-oo-ros |
| Son tres con cincuenta | €3.50 | son tres kon theen-KWEN-tah |
| Son ocho con veinte | €8.20 | son OH-choh kon BAYN-teh |
| Son doce con noventa | €12.90 | son DOH-theh kon noh-BEN-tah |
| Son quince con ochenta | €15.80 | son KEEN-theh kon oh-CHEN-tah |
| Son veintitrés con cincuenta | €23.50 | son bayn-tee-TRES kon theen-KWEN-tah |
| Son cuarenta y dos | €42.00 | son kwah-REN-tah ee dohs |
| Son cincuenta y cinco con treinta | €55.30 | …theen-KWEN-tah ee THEEN-koh kon TRAYN-tah |

> **Key word:** ***con*** = "with" = the decimal point. Listen for it!

## 👂 What to listen for

When they tell you the price, hunt for these sounds:

1. **The first number** (the euros) — often said fast
2. **"con"** — signals cents are coming
3. **The second number** (the cents) — usually *cincuenta* (50), *veinte* (20), *ochenta* (80), or *noventa* (90)

> If you miss it: **¿Cuánto es?** (How much?) or **¿Perdona, cuánto?** (Sorry, how much?)

---

## 🔁 Quick recovery phrases

- ¿Cuánto es? — How much is it?
- ¿Me lo puede repetir? — Can you repeat that?
- Perdona, ¿cuánto has dicho? — Sorry, how much did you say?
- ¿Treinta? ¿Trece? — Thirty? Thirteen? *(when you're unsure)*
- ¿Me lo escribes? — Can you write it down for me?

Do 10 of these a day and you'll catch prices without thinking. 💶
