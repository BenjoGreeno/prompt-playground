# 🗣️ Free-Conversation Prompts (no scripts — improvise!)

Real conversations aren't scripted. Once the scripts feel comfy, do these **open-ended**
challenges with your travel buddy. One of you plays the local; the other improvises. No
peeking at scripts — struggle a little, that's the point. Then swap.

Rule: **stay in Spanish for the whole prompt**, even if it's broken. Use your page-07
lifelines (*"¿Cómo se dice…?", "más despacio"*) instead of switching to English.

---

## 🥉 Level 1 — Survivable (Day 4 morning)

1. **The 60-second intro.** Introduce yourself: name, where you're from, why you're in
   Valencia, how long, whether you like it. Partner asks one follow-up.
2. **Order a full breakfast** from your imaginary partner-waiter, including one change
   ("no sugar", "oat milk"), and pay.
3. **You're lost.** Ask your partner for directions to the Mercado Central. Make them
   give you 3 steps; you repeat them back to confirm.
4. **Small talk with a taxi driver:** where you're from, how long the ride is, one
   comment about the city.

---

## 🥈 Level 2 — Stretchy (Day 4 afternoon)

5. **The paella debate.** Partner (as a proud local) insists seafood paella is best.
   You (having read Script 3 😏) defend *paella valenciana*. Keep it going 5 exchanges.
6. **Recommend me something.** Ask a local for their favourite bar/beach/dish and *why*.
   React with page-07 reaction words. Ask a follow-up.
7. **Something went wrong.** Explain a problem at the hotel and ask for it to be fixed —
   without using the exact script wording.
8. **Make a plan together.** Decide with your partner (in Spanish) what to do tomorrow:
   beach or old town? Morning or afternoon? Agree on a time.

---

## 🥇 Level 3 — Show off (if you're feeling brave)

9. **Tell a mini story:** describe something you did today in the past-ish
   (*"hoy he ido a la playa y he comido paella"*). Doesn't need to be grammatically perfect.
10. **Opinions:** partner asks *"¿Qué te ha parecido Valencia?"* (What did you think of
    Valencia?). Give 3 reasons you like it, using *porque* (because).
11. **The full café rush:** partner is a busy, fast-talking waiter who tries to switch to
    English. Your mission: keep them in Spanish and complete your order. 😅
12. **Teach your partner** one thing about themselves you learned — forces you to use *tú*
    ("you") forms and recall what they said.

---

## 🎲 Conversation dice (roll / pick randomly for a curveball)

Pick a **who**, a **topic**, and a **twist**:

**WHO:** waiter · shopkeeper · taxi driver · friendly stranger on the beach · hotel receptionist
**TOPIC:** food · directions · the weather · recommendations · a small problem · weekend plans
**TWIST:** they speak too fast · they only have change for €50 · they're out of what you want ·
they ask *you* a question back · they compliment your Spanish

> Example roll: *shopkeeper + a small problem + they speak too fast* → improvise it!

---

## 📈 Score yourselves (optional, but motivating)

After each prompt, give each other +1 point for every time you:
- stayed in Spanish instead of switching to English,
- used a filler word naturally (*pues…, a ver…*),
- used *"¿cómo se dice?"* to learn a new word live,
- recovered from being lost without freezing.

Highest score buys the next round of *agua de Valencia*. 🍹
