# 🧱 Lego Spanish — Sentence Builder

The cheat code for beginners: don't memorise 500 sentences. Memorise **~12 frames** and
slot vocab into them. This turns a small vocabulary into hundreds of real sentences.

Each frame is `START + [slot]`. Learn the START by heart; swap the `[slot]`.

---

## The 12 power frames

| Frame (memorise this) | Means | Example |
|-----------------------|-------|---------|
| **Quiero** [thing/verb] | I want… | *Quiero un café* — I want a coffee |
| **Quería** [thing] | I'd like… (politer) | *Quería la cuenta* — I'd like the bill |
| **¿Puedes** [verb]**?** | Can you…? | *¿Puedes ayudarme?* — Can you help me? |
| **¿Puedo** [verb]**?** | Can I…? | *¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta?* — Can I pay by card? |
| **¿Dónde está** [place]**?** | Where is…? | *¿Dónde está el baño?* — Where's the toilet? |
| **¿Tienes / Tienen** [thing]**?** | Do you have…? | *¿Tienen horchata?* — Do you have horchata? |
| **¿Cuánto cuesta** [thing]**?** | How much is…? | *¿Cuánto cuesta esto?* — How much is this? |
| **Me gusta** [thing] | I like… | *Me gusta la paella* — I like paella |
| **No entiendo** [thing] | I don't understand… | *No entiendo esta palabra* — …this word |
| **¿Cómo se dice** [word]**?** | How do you say…? | *¿Cómo se dice "beach"?* |
| **Necesito** [thing/verb] | I need… | *Necesito una farmacia* — I need a pharmacy |
| **¿Hay** [thing] **por aquí?** | Is there … nearby? | *¿Hay un cajero por aquí?* — an ATM nearby? |

> **Practice game (two people):** Person A calls out an English need ("ask if they have
> oat milk"), Person B has 5 seconds to build it from a frame:
> *"¿Tienen leche de avena?"* Swap and repeat. Fast, fun, addictive.

## The slot vocab bank (mix & match)

**Things (nouns)**
- un café · una cerveza · agua · la cuenta · el menú · una mesa
- el baño · una farmacia · un cajero · un taxi · el metro · una habitación
- esto (this) · eso (that) ← *point and say these when you don't know the word!*

**Actions (verbs — use after ¿Puedo/¿Puedes)**
- ayudarme (help me) · pagar (pay) · repetir (repeat) · esperar (wait)
- entrar (go in) · pasar (come through) · reservar (book) · ver (see/look)

**Politeness boosters (add to ANY sentence)**
- …por favor — please · …, ¿verdad? — right? · Perdona… — excuse me…

---

## ⚡ The "point + esto" hack

Don't know a word? **Point at it** and say:

- Quiero **esto**, por favor — I want **this**, please
- ¿Qué es **esto**? — What is this?
- **Esto**, ¿cómo se dice? — This, how do you say it?

You can survive an entire trip with *esto*, a pointing finger, and a smile. 😄

---

## Level-up connectors (glue two ideas together)

Once frames feel easy, join them:

- **y** — and · **pero** — but · **porque** — because · **también** — also
- **primero… y después…** — first… and then…
- *Quiero un café **y** una tostada, **por favor**.*
- *Me gusta, **pero** es un poco caro.* — I like it, but it's a bit expensive.
