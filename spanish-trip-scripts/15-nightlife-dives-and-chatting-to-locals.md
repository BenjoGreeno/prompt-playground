# 🎸 Nightlife, Dive Bars & Chatting to Locals

For dive bars, DIY spots and small grotty music venues — where you'll meet the *real*
Valencia. The Spanish here is faster and slangier, but people are usually warm. This page
covers **the fun stuff**, **what to do when a local chats to the two of you**, plus a
just-in-case section on **handling anyone who gets lairy** — calmly and safely.

---

## 🍻 Part 1 — Bar & gig know-how (Valencia)

### Where the good grotty stuff is
- **Barrio del Carmen** — the old town; narrow streets, dive bars, alternative spots.
- **Ruzafa (Russafa)** — hip, arty, lots of small bars and live music.
- **El Cabanyal** — the old fishermen's quarter near the beach; gritty, characterful, up-and-coming.
- Look for posters saying **"concierto"** (gig), **"directo"** (live), **"pinchada / DJ"**.

### Ordering like a regular (not a tourist)
- ¿Me pones una caña? — Can you get me a small draught beer? *(the local default)*
- Ponme un tercio. — Give me a bottle (330ml). `[TER-thee-oh]`
- Un cubata. — A spirit + mixer (rum & coke etc.). `[koo-BAH-tah]`
- ¿Qué tenéis de grifo? — What's on tap? `[keh teh-NAYS deh GREE-foh]`
- Lo mismo otra vez. — The same again.
- Cuando puedas, sin prisa. — Whenever you can, no rush. *(polite in a busy bar)*

### Gig / venue words you'll hear
- la entrada — the ticket / cover charge · ¿Hay entrada? — Is there a cover charge?
- la consumición — a drink often included with entry ("con consumición" = drink included)
- el grupo / la banda — the band · el escenario — the stage
- el bis / otra! — encore / "one more!" · flipante — mind-blowing (slang)
- el garito — the joint/dive (affectionate slang for a small bar) `[gah-REE-toh]`
- el after — the late-late place you go after closing

### Small talk that makes you a mate
- ¿Habéis visto a este grupo antes? — Have you (all) seen this band before?
- ¡Qué buen ambiente hay aquí! — What a great vibe in here! `[keh bwen am-bee-EN-teh]`
- ¿Qué música ponen normalmente? — What music do they usually play?
- ¿Conocéis algún garito con música en directo? — Know any dives with live music?
- Somos de fuera, ¿qué nos recomendáis? — We're from out of town, what do you recommend?

> **DIY / scene bonus:** *autogestionado* = self-run/DIY (you'll see it on posters for
> squat-y/collective venues). *"¿Es un sitio autogestionado?"* = "Is it a DIY/collective place?"
> Scene folks will warm to you instantly for knowing the word. 🤘

## 👥 Part 1.5 — When someone strikes up a chat with the TWO of you

Very common in a friendly bar: a local (or a group) starts talking **to both of you**.
They'll use ***vosotros*** ("you" plural) — listen for the *-áis / -éis* verb endings and
words aimed at "you two."

**🔊 Things they'll say to the pair of you:**
- **¿De dónde sois (vosotros)?** — Where are you two from? → *(see page 13)*
- **¿Sois pareja?** — Are you two a couple? → *Sí, somos pareja.* / *Sí, es mi novia.*
- **¿Estáis de vacaciones?** — Are you on holiday? → *Sí, una semana.*
- **¿Qué hacéis en Valencia?** — What are you doing in Valencia? → *Turismo, comer y beber.* 😄
- **¿Os gusta el grupo / la música?** — Do you two like the band/music? → *¡Nos encanta!*
- **¿Queréis algo? / ¿Os pongo algo?** — Do you want something? / Shall I get you something?
  → *No, gracias, acabamos de pedir.* — No thanks, we just ordered.
- **¿Os invito a una copa?** — Can I buy you two a drink? → *¡Qué majo/a! Vale, gracias.*
  (How nice of you! OK, thanks) — *or politely decline:* *Muy amable, pero estamos bien.*

**✅ Replying AS a pair (use "nosotros" / "we"):**
- Somos de Inglaterra. — We're from England.
- Estamos de vacaciones. — We're on holiday.
- Nos encanta Valencia. — We love Valencia.
- Somos pareja. — We're a couple.
- Ella habla mejor español que yo. — She speaks better Spanish than me. *(loops to your joke!)*

**Include your partner / bounce it over:**
- Ella es mi novia. — She's my girlfriend.
- Pregúntale a ella, habla tres idiomas. — Ask her, she speaks three languages. 😄
- ¿Verdad, cariño? — Right, love? *(turning to her — buys you time too)* `[kah-REEN-yoh]`

**Keeping a group chat alive (you don't need to catch every word):**
- ¿Y vosotros, sois de aquí? — And you lot, are you from here?
- ¿Nos recomendáis otro sitio? — Can you recommend us another place?
- Perdona, ¿a quién le toca? — Sorry, whose round/turn is it? *(fun in a group)*

> **Tip:** when it's the two of you, you can **tag-team** — if you get stuck, glance at your
> girlfriend and say *"¿Cómo se dice…?"* or *"Díselo tú"* (You tell them). Sharing the load
> keeps the conversation flowing and takes the pressure off. 👫

## 😅 Part 2 — Friendly-but-INTENSE (the loud, over-the-top local)

Often someone "talking the ear off you" isn't aggressive — they're just **loud, drunk-happy,
and being friendly**. Roll with it:

- ¡Tío/Tía, no te entiendo, pero me caes bien! — Mate, I don't get you, but I like you!
  `[TEE-oh/TEE-ah, noh teh en-tee-EN-doh, PEH-roh meh KAH-es bee-EN]`
- Más despacio, ¡que soy guiri! — Slower, I'm a foreigner! *(guiri = friendly slang for a
  Northern-European tourist; owning it is charming)* `[GEE-ree]`
- ¡Vale, vale, tú mandas! — OK OK, you're the boss!
- No te sigo, pero ¡salud! — I'm not following, but cheers! 🍻
- ¿Me lo explicas otra vez? Me he perdido. — Explain it again? I got lost.

> These keep it light and let you stay in a chaotic-friendly chat without stress.

## 🛑 Part 3 — If someone gets LAIRY / aggressive (stay calm & safe)

**Priority = your safety, not your Spanish.** Don't argue, don't match their energy. Stay
calm, be polite, create distance, leave. These lines buy you space.

### De-escalate (calm, non-confrontational)
- Tranquilo/a, no busco problemas. — Easy, I'm not looking for trouble.
  `[tran-KEE-loh/lah, noh BOOS-koh proh-BLEH-mas]`
- Perdona si te he molestado. — Sorry if I bothered you. *(defuses, even if you did nothing)*
- No quiero discutir. — I don't want to argue. `[noh kee-EH-roh dees-koo-TEER]`
- Todo bien, tranquilo. — It's all fine, take it easy.
- Vale, tienes razón. — OK, you're right. *(sometimes just agreeing ends it fastest)*

### Create distance / exit
- Nos vamos ya. — We're leaving now.
- Déjanos en paz, por favor. — Leave us alone, please. `[DEH-hah-nos en pas]`
- No te acerques. — Don't come closer. *(firm)* `[noh teh ah-THER-kes]`
- Voy a hablar con el camarero. — I'm going to speak to the bartender.

### Get help (say loudly & clearly)
- ¡Necesito ayuda! — I need help!
- Llama a seguridad, por favor. — Call security, please. *(to staff)*
- ¡Este hombre me está molestando! — This man is bothering me! *(to staff/crowd — gets attention)*
- Llama a la policía. — Call the police.

### Words you might HEAR if someone's kicking off (recognise the danger)
- ¿Qué miras? — "What are you looking at?" *(classic confrontation opener — don't take the bait)*
- ¿Tienes algún problema? — "You got a problem?"
- Vete / Fuera de aquí. — "Go / Get out of here."
- → **Best response to all of these:** *"No, ningún problema. Ya nos vamos."* (No, no
  problem at all. We're leaving now.) + walk away.

> **Golden rules:** (1) Never argue back or defend your "honour" — just leave.
> (2) A guiri who stays calm and polite almost never gets hurt. (3) Bartenders and door
> staff (*seguridad*) are your allies — get to them.

## 📞 Part 4 — Emergencies (memorise this number!)

- **112** — the all-purpose EU emergency number (police, ambulance, fire). English-speaking
  operators are usually available. Works from any phone, even locked.
- la policía — police · una ambulancia — an ambulance · seguridad — venue security/bouncers
- Me han robado. — I've been robbed. `[meh an rroh-BAH-doh]`
- He perdido a mi novia / a mi amigo. — I've lost my girlfriend / my friend.
- ¿Dónde está el hospital más cercano? — Where's the nearest hospital?

> Save **112** in your phones now, and agree a **meeting point** with your girlfriend in
> case you get separated in a busy venue. Simple, and it saves a lot of panic. 🙂

---

## 🎯 The 4 to keep in your back pocket

1. **¡Más despacio, que soy guiri!** — friendly, defuses & charms *(everyday use)*
2. **No busco problemas, ya nos vamos.** — calm exit from any tension
3. **Déjanos en paz, por favor.** — firm "leave us alone"
4. **112** — emergency number *(hopefully never needed!)*

Have a brilliant, safe night out — the grotty venues are where Valencia's heart is. 🎸🍻
