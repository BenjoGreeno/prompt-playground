# 🎣 Keep the Conversation Alive (the most important page)

Your goal isn't perfect Spanish — it's **staying in the conversation** so natives keep
talking to you (and you keep learning). Locals switch to English the moment you freeze or
go silent. These phrases stop that from happening.

---

## 1. The golden opener (say this early!)

Tell people you're learning. It changes *everything* — they slow down, help you, and
usually don't switch to English.

- Estoy aprendiendo español, ¿puedes hablar despacio? — I'm learning Spanish, can you speak slowly?
  `[es-TOY ah-pren-dee-EN-doh es-pan-YOL, PWEH-des ah-BLAR des-PAH-thee-oh]`
- Perdona mi español — Sorry about my Spanish `[per-DOH-nah mee es-pan-YOL]`
- ¿Puedes ayudarme a practicar? — Can you help me practise? `[PWEH-des ah-yoo-DAR-meh ah prak-tee-KAR]`

---

## 2. Filler words (buy thinking time — sound fluent!)

Sprinkle these while your brain catches up. Natives use them constantly.

- Pues… — Well… / Um… `[pwes]`
- A ver… — Let's see… `[ah ber]`
- Es que… — It's just that… / The thing is… `[es keh]`
- O sea… — I mean… `[oh SEH-ah]`
- Bueno… — Well… / OK… `[BWEH-noh]`
- ¿Cómo se dice…? — How do you say…? `[KOH-moh seh DEE-theh]`
- ¿Sabes? — You know? `[SAH-bes]`
- Vale, vale — OK, OK `[BAH-leh]`

> **Trick:** Learn to say *"Pues… a ver…"* smoothly. It buys ~3 seconds and makes you
> sound like you're *thinking in Spanish*, not panicking. 😌

---

## 3. Reaction words (show you're listening — keeps them talking)

You don't need to reply in full sentences. React, and they'll keep going.

- ¡Ah, vale! — Ah, OK! · ¡Qué bien! — How nice!
- ¿En serio? — Really? · ¡Qué guay! — How cool!
- Claro — Of course · Exacto — Exactly
- ¡Qué fuerte! — Wow / No way! · Vaya… — Wow / Oh dear…
- Tiene sentido — That makes sense · Entiendo — I understand

---

## 4. The "I'm lost, help me" survival set (never freeze!)

When you don't understand, DON'T go silent. Say one of these:

- Perdona, no te he entendido — Sorry, I didn't understand you `[per-DOH-nah, noh teh eh en-ten-DEE-doh]`
- ¿Puedes repetir, por favor? — Can you repeat, please?
- ¿Más despacio, por favor? — More slowly, please?
- ¿Qué significa…? — What does … mean? `[keh seeg-nee-FEE-kah]`
- ¿Cómo se escribe? — How's it spelled? `[KOH-moh seh es-KREE-beh]`
- ¿Puedes escribirlo? — Can you write it down?
- Una palabra no más — Just one word (i.e. "give me the key word")

---

## 5. Redirect the conversation (steer to what you CAN say)

- Cambiando de tema… — Changing the subject… `[kam-bee-AN-doh deh TEH-mah]`
- Por cierto… — By the way… `[por thee-ER-toh]`
- ¿Y tú? — And you? (the ultimate lazy-genius reply — bounce it back!)
- Cuéntame más — Tell me more `[KWEN-tah-meh mas]`
- ¿Qué me recomiendas? — What do you recommend? (great with locals — they love this)

---

## 6. The anti-English-switch phrase

When they hear your accent and start speaking English, say (with a smile):

- ¿Podemos seguir en español? Quiero practicar. — Can we continue in Spanish? I want to practise.
  `[poh-DEH-mos seh-GEER en es-pan-YOL? kee-EH-roh prak-tee-KAR]`

Most people will happily oblige and slow down for you. 🙌

---

## 🎯 The 5 phrases to burn into your brain first

If you learn nothing else, learn these — they unlock everything:

1. **¿Cómo se dice ___?** — How do you say ___? *(learn new words live)*
2. **¿Qué significa ___?** — What does ___ mean? *(understand new words live)*
3. **Más despacio, por favor** — Slower, please *(stay in it)*
4. **Estoy aprendiendo español** — I'm learning Spanish *(get them on your side)*
5. **¿Y tú?** — And you? *(keep the ball in their court)*
